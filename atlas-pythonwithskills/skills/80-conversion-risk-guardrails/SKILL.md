# Conversion guardrails: concentration and cash

## What it changes
- **Conversion map:** the "high cash" program note appears when cash and cash alternatives are 15% of the account or more (was 25%).
- **Conversion map:** each cell that sells a concentrated position at a gain gets a Risk note: get the client's written acknowledgment of the tax cost, or stage the sale with TET.

## Why
The brochure says the program is not designed for concentrated or high-cash clients, but says nothing about where the lines are. Risk Management sets the lines.
