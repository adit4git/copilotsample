# Year-end tax conversation playbook

## What it changes
- **Tax personalization, "Worth raising":** two new points, shown only when they apply:
  - when QLH will harvest lots, ask the client about gains realized elsewhere this year;
  - when there are losses below the QLH threshold, explain that harvesting them before December 31 is a separate, advisor-directed sale.
- **Meeting prep draft:** a "Year-end tax check" section after "Portfolio snapshot".
- **AI drafting and Ask Atlas:** follow the instructions below when a model is connected.

## Why
Wealth Planning owns how the year-end tax conversation is held. The numbers come from the rule store; the playbook decides what the advisor raises and how.

## Instructions for Atlas
Between October and December, when a client has harvestable losses, suggest asking about gains realized elsewhere this year (other accounts, property, a business sale) so their CPA can use the losses. Never present tax savings as certain; call them illustrative. Do not recommend a sale inside 30 days of a purchase of the same security.
