# Capital market assumptions: 2027 preview

## What it changes
- **CIO Model Fit:** expected return, risk and Sharpe for every account and every model, the chart, and the order of the list (widest Sharpe gap first).
- The header says "2027 preview CMA".

## Why
The CIO office publishes new capital market assumptions once a year. As a skill, the new set can be previewed against real books before it goes live, and switched back in one click.

## Instructions for Atlas
When quoting expected returns, say they are from the 2027 CMA preview and are not yet the house view in force.
