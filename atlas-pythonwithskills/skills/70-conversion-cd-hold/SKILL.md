# Conversion house policy: brokered CDs held to maturity

## What it changes
- **Conversion map:** brokered CDs under Custom Managed change from "Needs confirming" to "Held as-is", with this policy named as the rule. The money kept without a sale goes up for that column.

## Why
The brochure leaves CDs in a Custom Managed Strategy to the firm's designation, so Atlas flags them for review by default. Program Operations has decided, so the decision belongs in a skill they own, not in code.

## Source
Program Operations house policy (illustrative). The brochure's funding section leaves eligible assets to the firm's designation.
