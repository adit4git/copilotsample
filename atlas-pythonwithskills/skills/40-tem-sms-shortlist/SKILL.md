# TEM Style Manager shortlist: global and dividend sleeves

## What it changes
- **Tax personalization, Options tab:** the shortlist of TEM Style Manager Strategies. Atlas shows one pick per family instead of two. International is included once international equity reaches 5% of equity (was 15%). Global and U.S. dividend families are always offered.

## Why
Manager Research owns which strategies are put in front of clients. Changing the shortlist is a research decision, not a code change.

## Instructions for Atlas
When listing TEM Style Manager Strategies, say they are Manager Research's shortlist and that every strategy still has its own minimum.
