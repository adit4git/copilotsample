# Conversion playbook: managed first, within a tax budget

## What it changes
- **Conversion map:** the strategy type Atlas suggests and opens on. It tries Custom Managed, then Managed. It picks the first one whose estimated entry tax is at most 3.5% of the account. If neither fits, it falls back to the default: the type that keeps the most money invested without a sale.
- The reason is written out in plain words on the map and in Ask Atlas.

## Why
Out of the box, Atlas suggests the type that triggers the fewest sales. That is a neutral default, but the advisory program team wants professionally managed accounts first when the tax cost is small. That is a business choice, so it lives in a skill the team owns.

## Instructions for Atlas
When discussing a brokerage-to-advisory conversion, lead with the strategy type the conversion map suggests and give its reason. Always state the estimated entry tax and that it is illustrative. Never say a conversion has no tax cost if any sale is involved.
