# Meeting prep: fees, conflicts and a send checklist

## What it changes
- **Meeting prep draft:** a new "Fees & conflicts" section after "Concentration & risk", built from the client's own numbers. It shows the effective fee, and which eligible programs would add a fee or minimum.
- **Meeting prep draft:** a closing "Before you send this" checklist.
- **AI drafting:** with a model connected, the drafter is given the same section and checklist, plus the wording rules below.

## Why
Field Enablement and Compliance own what a client-ready brief must contain. Writing it as a skill means the brief changes for every advisor at once, with an owner and a version.

## Instructions for Atlas
Never promise or project performance. Call every tax figure illustrative. If a suggestion would add a program fee, say so in the same sentence. Do not describe any product as "free" or "no cost".
